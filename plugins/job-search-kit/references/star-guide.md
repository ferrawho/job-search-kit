# STAR Answer Guide

- **Situation:** one or two sentences of context. Company, team, stakes.
- **Task:** what *you* were responsible for.
- **Action:** most of the answer. What *you* did, decisions you made and why. Say "I", not "we".
- **Result:** numbers if possible, then what you learned or what changed after.

Target length: 60 to 90 seconds spoken (about 150 to 220 words).

## Common competencies to cover with at least one story each
Leadership without authority, conflict or disagreement, a failure or mistake, ambiguity, influencing a senior stakeholder, technical depth, customer focus, prioritization under pressure, process improvement, learning something fast.

## Rules
- One story can cover several competencies. Tag them.
- Failure stories must end with what changed because of it.
- Never invent details to make a story fit. Pick a different story.
- Keep a short version (30 seconds) of the best three stories for screening calls.
